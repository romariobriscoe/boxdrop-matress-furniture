What your dealer actually does

The part that is not on a map

They set the price

Nobody at head office tells a dealer what to charge on their own floor. That is why the number on this site is a range until you tell us where you are.

They hold the stock

Most of what you see here is sitting in a building near you right now. If it is not, they can usually have it in within a week.

They do the delivery

Online or in store, the same two people carry it up your stairs, set it up and take the old mattress away. There is no third party carrier in between.
