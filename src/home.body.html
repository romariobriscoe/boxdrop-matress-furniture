Why there are two prices

BoxDrop is 400 independently owned stores. This website is the national front door. The dealer is the business that actually holds the stock and drives the van, and that changes what each one costs to run.

Buy online, pay the price on this page

The convenient road. You never negotiate, you never drive anywhere, and the number does not move.

  • Ships to every address in the lower 48
  • A fixed delivery window you pick at checkout
  • Returns handled centrally, not store by store
  • One price for everyone, published

Buy local, pay less

Your dealer already owns the inventory, the building and the truck. Their cost to serve you is lower, so their floor price is lower.

  • Typically 12 to 18% under the online price
  • Lie on it first, in the size you are actually buying
  • Same day pickup on most queens in stock
  • They set the number, not us

Either way, the same dealer delivers

An online order does not go to a freight carrier. It goes to the BoxDrop nearest you, and they load it.

  • A BoxDrop crew, not a parcel service
  • Set up in the room you want it in
  • Old mattress hauled away at no charge
  • A local phone number if anything is wrong

Buying guide

How firm is firm?

Firmness is not quality. It is a preference, and the industry describes it with words that mean different things at every brand. Here is the scale BoxDrop uses on every product page, so a medium here is the same medium everywhere on this site.

1 Plush5 Medium10 Extra firm
3 to 4 · PlushSide sleepers, lighter frames, anyone with shoulder or hip pressure.
5 to 6 · MediumThe safe answer for couples who sleep differently. Two thirds of what we deliver.
7 to 8 · FirmBack and stomach sleepers, heavier frames, anyone who wakes up sinking.
